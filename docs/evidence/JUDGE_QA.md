# Judge Q&A

Each answer is tagged **[Measured]** (archived frozen result), **[Live demo]**, **[Design]** (property of the design/code) or **[Future work]**. Claim IDs refer to [`CLAIMS_REGISTRY.md`](CLAIMS_REGISTRY.md).

1. **What is new about MADQAQ Sentinel?** [Design] It checks procedure integrity by cross-checking what a camera physically observed against what a digital record claims, so it can name the exact step and kind of inconsistency. [Measured] In the frozen synthetic benchmark only the dual-channel configuration detected phantom completion and channel contradiction (C008).
2. **Why do you need two channels?** [Measured] Internal ablations: physical-only and digital-only had recall 0.0 on phantom completion and channel contradiction, because these are defined by disagreement between channels (C008). Frozen synthetic benchmark.
3. **What happens if the camera misses a step?** [Design] The digital record may claim a step with no physical evidence; the engine then raises PHANTOM_COMPLETION, or UNCERTAIN when evidence is clearly insufficient. [Measured] This conservatism produced 60 false phantom alerts on weak physical evidence in the benchmark (C009).
4. **What is Phantom Completion?** [Design] A digital completion for a step with no supporting physical evidence. [Measured] Recall 1.000, precision 0.860, F1 0.925 on the frozen synthetic benchmark (C006).
5. **What is Channel Contradiction?** [Design] Physical evidence and digital status contradict each other at a step. [Measured] Precision/recall/F1 1.000 within the frozen synthetic benchmark; generated contradictions are clean by construction (C007).
6. **What does 95% mean?** [Measured] 95.0% of the 1,200 frozen synthetic journeys had a predicted violation set exactly equal to the oracle's (95% bootstrap CI 0.9375–0.9625). It is not camera or real-world accuracy (C002).
7. **Is 98.1% your accuracy?** No. [Measured] 0.981 is Macro-F1 (CI 0.976–0.986), the mean of per-violation-type F1, on the frozen synthetic benchmark (C003).
8. **Is the benchmark real-world data?** No. [Design] It is 1,200 procedurally generated synthetic journeys with ground truth from an independent oracle over a latent world, not from the engine (C001).
9. **Is the digital channel real?** No. [Live demo] It is a local simulator, always labelled SIMULATED DIGITAL CHANNEL. There is no integration with any checkpoint or government system (C011).
10. **Does the system use facial recognition?** No. [Design] The prototype has no face detection, recognition or biometric identity; it detects persons and tracks them with temporary IDs (C012).
11. **Does it identify people permanently?** No. [Design] Only a temporary tracker ID and a random journey UUID in memory, lost on restart or reset. This is a design property, not a certified privacy guarantee (C012).
12. **What is the biggest current limitation?** [Measured] Weak physical evidence: 60 low-confidence cases caused false phantom-completion alerts (C009). Also: synthetic benchmark only, simulated digital channel, no measured camera accuracy.
13. **Why did the system make 60 mistakes?** [Measured] When a step was observed only below the confidence threshold but recorded digitally, the engine treated the observation as absent and raised PHANTOM_COMPLETION where the oracle expects UNCERTAIN. All 60 are one scenario family. This reflects conservative evidence handling and has not been validated as an operationally optimal policy. We disclosed it and changed neither engine nor oracle after seeing it. [Future work] Calibrated handling of sub-threshold evidence.
14. **Why not simply trust the digital system?** [Measured] The digital-only ablation left 231 of 812 violated journeys without an alarm and cannot detect phantom completion or contradiction (C008). [Design] A digital record alone cannot reveal that it disagrees with physical reality.
15. **Why not use cameras only?** [Measured] The physical-only ablation left 140 of 812 violated journeys without an alarm and cannot detect phantom completion or contradiction (C008). Cameras alone cannot see what the record claims.
16. **Is the system production ready?** No. It is a research prototype, working proof of concept and finalist demonstration prototype. Production readiness is not supported by the evidence (C015).
17. **What would be needed before deployment?** [Future work] Field trials with measured camera accuracy, real digital-record integration, a calibrated policy for weak evidence, privacy/legal review, and validation on non-synthetic data. None of this has been done.
18. **What exactly was tested live?** [Live demo] The webcam pipeline turning observations into temporary physical events, combined with a SIMULATED DIGITAL CHANNEL on the dashboard. No accuracy figure is attached to the live demo (C010, C011).
19. **What exactly was tested synthetically?** [Measured] The deterministic engine and three comparison systems on 1,200 frozen procedurally generated journeys, evaluated once; engine evaluation took tens of microseconds per case — ENGINE EVALUATION ONLY, not system or camera latency (C001–C009, C013).
20. **How do you prevent misleading evidence claims?** [Design] Every claim lives in one registry with allowed and forbidden wording, tied to archived hash-checked files. A read-only checker (`python -m apps.evidence.main check`) verifies the numbers and scans documents for prohibited phrases. The final evaluation was run once and frozen (C014 notes the final split shares the development generator design).
